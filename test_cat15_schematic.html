Working Summary
✓BME280 measures ambient temperature & humidity via I²C bus.
✓Soil moisture sensor reads volumetric soil moisture as analog voltage (ADC1_IN0).
✓STM32 processes sensor readings and uploads telemetry via ESP8266 using MQTT over Wi-Fi.
✓Relay actuates DC water pump automatically based on soil moisture threshold or manual Node-RED command.
